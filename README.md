# QuickScan

A modern QR scanner and generator app built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3.

QuickScan is designed to make scanning, creating, saving, and managing QR codes simple and fast. I designed and developed the complete application with a responsive UI that adapts across different screen sizes.

## Features

- Scan QR codes using the camera
- Scan QR codes from images in the gallery
- Generate QR codes for:
  - Text
  - URLs
  - Wi-Fi
  - Contacts
  - Email
  - Phone numbers
- Preview generated QR codes
- Save and share generated QR codes
- View scan history
- Save important QR results for quick access
- Manage created QR codes
- Clean and responsive Material 3 interface
- Dedicated QR scanner interface with flashlight and gallery support

## Built With

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Material 3
- Modern Android development tools

## My Role

I designed and developed the complete application independently, including the UI, interactions, QR scanning and generation experience, and responsive layouts.

The interface was designed to work smoothly across different screen sizes while keeping the experience simple and consistent.
